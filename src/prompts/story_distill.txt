Rules:
- Cover ONE story: the one the topic names. Ignore unrelated stories or page chrome in the text.
- Use only what the source text supports. No outside facts, no guesses, no invented numbers.
- Write everything in English, even if the source is Arabic. Keep names, numbers and dates exact.
- "hook": one punchy sentence (≤ 20 words) that would stop a viewer scrolling. No clickbait lies.
- "key_facts": 3 to 5 short standalone facts, most important first.
- "claims": specific assertions a viewer might doubt (figures, quotes, causes), each with the
  outlet that reported it; [] if there are none.
- "why_trending": one sentence on why people care right now.
- "usable": false if the text doesn't actually tell a story (paywall stub, bare headlines with
  no facts, unrelated page) — then explain in "reason". Otherwise true.

Respond with JSON only, in exactly this shape:
