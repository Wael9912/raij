Structure, as ordered beats:

2. "body": 3 to 6 SECTIONS. Each section starts on a beat that carries "chapter": a 2–5 word Arabic chapter

3. "payoff": 1–2 beats — the answer to the hook's question (the thesis), and why it matters to the listener.
4. "cta": 1 short line tied to the story, in the spirit of the closing line for the series you pick
